EP11 · Quantization · where every number on screen comes from

SIZES ARE REAL FILES (ep11_packed.py, ep11_packed_output.txt)
The bot is saved to disk as packed 8-bit / 4-bit / 2-bit files (weights packed into bytes + their scales), the file size is measured,
then the bot is loaded back from each file and asked the 14 chats it never trained on:
- 32-bit original: 497.8 MB file
- 16-bit: 249.2 MB, 14 of 14 replies same as the original bot
- 8-bit, one scale per column: 125.2 MB (3.98x smaller, said as "four times"), 14 of 14 same
- 4-bit blocks (groups of 32) + 8-bit word table: 95.7 MB (5.2x, said as "five times"), 8 of 14 same, refund answer unchanged
- 2-bit blocks + 8-bit word table: 74.5 MB, 0 of 14 ("myappon. You can be sent.", "Pay on. Pay on. Pay.")
To run, weights are unpacked back to 32-bit numbers: these are storage savings, not runtime memory or speed.
"Same" = the exact same reply as the original bot on the 14 held-out chats. It is a quick check, not a full quality test.

QUALITY EXPERIMENTS (ep11_agent_quant.py; same rounding, applied in memory)
- 8-bit, ONE scale per whole table: 3 of 14 same
- 4-bit, one scale per table: answers with commas (",,,,,,,")
- 4-bit groups of 32 on everything incl. the word table: "Go to the refund, and choose the refund that bests the refund."
(The packed files above reproduce the 8-bit, 4-bit mix and 2-bit results exactly.)
